Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Shorts Automation ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you purchase a license.
  • Payment information: subscription payments are handled entirely by our payment processor, Paddle. We do not collect or store your card details.
  • License usage: basic activation and usage data needed to validate your license.

Because the Software runs on your own machine and connects directly to your own Google OAuth credentials, we do not receive, host, or store your YouTube channel data, videos, or Google account tokens. That access happens entirely on your own hardware, between your machine and Google's servers.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above to process your subscription, validate your license, and provide customer support. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. YouTube API Services and Google user data

The Software's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. All YouTube channel data is accessed and processed locally on your own machine; it is not transmitted to us. You can revoke the Software's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

4. Data retention

We retain account and billing data for as long as your license is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 6).

5. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of third-party providers, including Paddle (subscription billing). These providers process data under their own privacy policies. The Software itself connects to Google/YouTube directly from your machine.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at batub8500@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to batub8500@gmail.com.
