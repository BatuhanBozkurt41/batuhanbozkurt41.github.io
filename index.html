What it does

A full content pipeline, not just a script generator.

🔎 Real topic research

Pulls trending, verifiable topics for your channel's niche instead of generic filler — sports records, archaeological finds, game news, whatever your niche is.

✍️ Script generation

Writes a short, fact-based narration script sized for a 45–90 second video, matching your channel's tone.

🗣️ Voice-over

Local text-to-speech with a large voice library — no per-minute cloud TTS billing.

🖼️ Visuals

Sources relevant photos or video clips from licensed stock sources (or generates them), then composes a finished vertical video with captions and music.

✅ Human approval, via Telegram

Every video is sent to you as a Telegram message with Approve/Reject buttons before it ever touches your channel — full automation stays optional, not forced.

📊 Control panel

A web dashboard to connect YouTube accounts, manage channels, schedule publish times, and watch the pipeline's status in real time.

How it works

Set up once, then it runs on your own schedule.

Connect your YouTube channel

A guided setup wizard walks you through creating a Google OAuth client and connecting each channel — no manual file editing.

Connect Telegram

Paste a bot token from BotFather. This is how you'll approve, reject, or get notified about every video.

Define your channels and schedule

Pick a niche, a voice, and publish times. The pipeline proposes an idea at each scheduled time.

Approve from your phone

Get a Telegram message with the finished video. Tap Approve and it uploads — tap Reject and a new idea is generated automatically.
